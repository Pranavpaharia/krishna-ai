The Problem & Dilemma

Why Generic AI Fails At Sacred Counsel

When people face severe anxiety, grief, and moral paralysis, hallucination destroys trust.

Generic LLMs (Black Box)

  • Invent fake verse chapters and confabulate quotes
  • Blend incompatible philosophical traditions carelessly
  • Give generic, shallow platitudes for heavy grief and burnout
  • Send sensitive personal confessions to proprietary cloud servers

Krishna AI (Our Solution)

  • Zero Hallucination: Only cites ingested canonical verses
  • Verbatim Verification: Machine-checked character-by-character
  • Empathetic 5-Part Counsel: Root cause, verse, and practical action
  • Privacy Sovereignty: Meta open weights run on private edge hardware
Under The Hood

End-to-End Grounded Architecture

Bridging 5,000 years of classical texts to modern dilemmas with Meta's reasoning engine.

Meta Reasoning

Interactive UI
FastAPI backend + responsive web chat with verifiable citation accordion.
