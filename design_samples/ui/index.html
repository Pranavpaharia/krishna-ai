Grounded on two books

Ask, and be still

You

Any advice on the stock tips a friend sent me?

Krishna

The teachings do not speak of this. I will not invent a verse to answer it. The nearest teaching is this: one who acts without clinging to the result is not shaken by gain or loss — SB 11.8.11, and BG 9.31 holds the same shape. That is about your attachment to the outcome, not about the tip.

Reference

BG 2.47

कर्मण्येवाधिकारस्ते मा फलेषु कदाचन।

You have a right to perform your prescribed duty, but you are not entitled to the fruits of action.
