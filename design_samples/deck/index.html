A chat that speaks in Krishna's voice and can only quote the two books it was given.

Open on the claim, not on the tech. Traceable is the product — everything else is plumbing.

The problem

A confident answer is not a trustworthy answer.

A language model will hand you a verse number that does not exist in the book, and it will sound exactly like a verse that does. For a reader who wants to open the page and check, that is the whole product risk.

Frame the risk in the reader's words: "can I open the PDF at this page and see the same sentence?"

Grounded corpus

1,764records

700 verses from Bhagavad-gītā As It Is and 1,064 from The Uddhava-gītā, extracted page by page and embedded at 2048-dim.

1,034 PDF pages → 700 verses; 929 pages → 1,064 records. Nothing was typed in by hand. Chapter 1 ends at 1.46 in this edition — no 1.47 record exists, because inventing one would break the grounding rule.

How a question travels

Four stages, one of them adversarial.

01

Extract

Parse the printed verse markers and purports into ref, translation, purport, PDF page.

02

Embed

Every translation embedded through the gateway; row alignment asserted at load.

03

Retrieve

Hybrid: embedding + idf-keyword + an inspectable topic map. Top six passages, verbatim.

04

Verify

Checker confirms each reference exists and each quote matches character for character.

Stage 04 is the differentiator. Stages 01–03 are commodity RAG; 04 is the enforcement layer.

Citation enforcement

Three rules the answer cannot break.

The reference must exist

Every cited id is looked up in the store. A number the model invented is removed before the answer is shown.

The quote must match

Quoted text is compared to the stored translation character for character. Paraphrase is labelled as paraphrase.

Failure stays visible

On a miss: one regeneration with the errors named, then the fabricated parts are stripped. The reader sees what was stripped.

Say the failure path out loud — it is the honest part. The UI must show a degraded answer as degraded, not polish it.

This is the strongest slide: enforcement is not a filter that hides answers, it is a filter that tells the truth about coverage.

Design pairing

warm-editorial for the surface, kami for the print.

The chat already exposes a semantic token layer, so pairing is a remap — not a rewrite. Display stays serif, elevation drops to a ring, accent drops from six uses per screen to two.

Surfaces go warm

Cool navy replaced by warm parchment and warm charcoal. No pure black, no pure white.

Elevation flattens

22px blur becomes a 1px ring; raised shadow only on hover. Radius capped at 24px.

Devanāgarī gets its own leading

Georgia carries no Devanagari glyphs — a named face plus 1.7 body leading before the serif fallback.

Citation state is the one place accent is allowed to work: verified, partial, stripped. Common Region padding ≥16px, 8–12px inside a group, 32–48px between groups.
